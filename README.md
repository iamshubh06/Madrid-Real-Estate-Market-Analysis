#  Madrid Real Estate Market Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Madrid's real estate market using Power BI to identify pricing trends, key price drivers, and investment opportunities. The analysis focuses on property characteristics, market segmentation, and price efficiency to support data-driven decision-making for buyers and investors.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The goal of this project is to answer the following questions:

- What factors influence property prices in Madrid?
- How do property features impact buy prices?
- Which property types provide better investment value?
- How can price efficiency be used to identify investment opportunities?

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Market Overview
Provides a high-level view of the Madrid housing market.

**KPIs:**
- Total Listings
- Average Buy Price
- Average Price per Square Meter
- Average Loan Interest Rate

**Key Insight:**
- Madrid properties have an average buy price of approximately **€636K** and an average price per square meter of **€4.03K**.

---

### 2. Property Condition & Quality Analysis

Analyzes property condition and development status.

**Key Findings:**
- Resale properties account for **88.64%** of listings.
- New developments represent only **6.80%** of the market.
- Ready-to-move properties dominate the market.
- Exterior properties are more common than interior properties.

---

### 3. Feature Impact on Property Prices

Evaluates how property features affect buy prices.

**Key Findings:**
- Properties with pools average **€0.30M**, compared to **€0.21M** without pools.
- Properties with gardens average **€1.83M**, compared to **€0.21M** without gardens.
- New developments average **€0.27M**, compared to **€0.23M** for resale properties.
- Premium amenities contribute significantly to higher property values.

---

### 4. Market Segmentation & Investment Opportunities

Segments the market by property type and analyzes investment potential.

**Key Findings:**
- Áticos show the highest average price per square meter at approximately **€4.9K/sq.m**.
- Casa o Chalet properties average around **€3.8K/sq.m**.
- Price efficiency varies significantly across property types.
- Higher-priced properties do not always offer better investment value.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Property features such as gardens, pools, and modern construction create clear price premiums.
- New developments command higher prices despite representing a small share of the market.
- Price per square meter is a more reliable investment metric than total property price.
- Market segmentation reveals hidden investment opportunities across different property types.
- Data-driven analysis helps identify value-focused investments rather than relying solely on headline prices.

---

## 💡 Conclusion

The Madrid real estate market is strongly influenced by property condition, development status, and premium features. By combining market segmentation with price-per-square-meter analysis, investors can identify more efficient opportunities and make informed real estate decisions.
